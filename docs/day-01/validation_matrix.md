# Validation matrix review

The machine-readable matrix is
[`data/processed/validation_matrix.csv`](../../data/processed/validation_matrix.csv).
Its 12 rows cover all article-reported combinations of F7-1/F7-2, MELCOR/
SPHINCS, and the three observable families. Values remain identified as
graphical time series because the article supplies no raw tables or numeric
error metrics; qualitative statements are transcribed separately.

The matrix records:

- measured observable and definition;
- experimental value or time-series identifier and units;
- the absence of measurement uncertainty and a fully defined time basis;
- code/version/case identity;
- predicted value or curve identifier and units;
- visual comparison as the only available comparison method;
- paper page and table/figure locator for both experiment and prediction;
- qualitative author conclusion, kept separate from numeric data.

STAR-CCM+ validation rows are intentionally absent because no simulations have
been executed. They may be added only after Day 1 approval and execution.
