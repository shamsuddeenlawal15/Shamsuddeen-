Days are work packages, not calendar promises. Day 6 is the current scope.
No subsequent package is authorized by scheduling it. Scientific review gates
take precedence over dates.

| Journal/submission | Assess scope, current Q1 category/year and journal requirements | Verified current ranking; reviewer approval before submission |

The detailed [Day 2 publication roadmap](../day-02/Updated_Publication_Roadmap.md)
remains the longer-term framework; this sequencing supersedes the immediate
work priority, not its evidence/validation requirements. Q1 is a target, not a
promised ranking or publication outcome.

H1 remains under Days 3–5 with its own synthetic geometry, material constants
and results schema. Source recovery and geometry documentation may proceed
while H1 awaits local evidence; physical F7 simulations must not skip numerical
verification. H1 passing would not establish sodium-model validity.

Potential original contributions remain conditional: oxide/spreading coupling,
thermal-contact sensitivity or ventilation/three-dimensional effects after
baseline reproduction. Do not select coefficients using the same final areas
then claim independent validation of those areas. Keep calibration, validation
and extension datasets/parameters explicitly separated.

If Day 7 cannot resolve head/port/pan geometry, remain at specification review
or seek approval for P0 as an explicitly reduced study. Do not “stay on schedule”
by inventing geometry or claiming full F7 reproduction.
